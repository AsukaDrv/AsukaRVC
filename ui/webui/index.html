Voice conversion options
Duet cover — two AI voices (optional)

The song is converted once per voice model, then both takes are woven into one duet vocal track with short crossfades. Auto mode detects the pauses between sung lines and switches voices there, so nobody sings over a word. The pitch offset shifts only the second voice (e.g. +12 to put a female model over a male original). The second model must differ from the main one.
